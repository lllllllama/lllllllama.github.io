Research

Depth-Dominant Skeleton Detection for Natural Scenes

First author · 2026 arXiv preprint

DDSkel uses depth as the primary input and RGB as an auxiliary signal to detect object skeletons in natural images. The paper reports F-measure comparisons on SK506, SK-LARGE, WH-SYMMAX, and SymPASCAL.
